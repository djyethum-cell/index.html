Only change if you understand Developer Options. This is not required for good aim.

Tip: change only one setting at a time. If your aim goes over the head, lower General or Red Dot by 3–5 points; if your drag feels slow, raise it by 3–5 and retest.

To test: enter Training Ground, use your usual weapon, practise short upward drags, and keep the setup that feels consistent.

💡 Quick tuning guide

• Aim passes above the head: reduce General / Red Dot slightly.
• Aim stops at the chest: increase General / Red Dot slightly.
• Scope aim shakes: lower the matching scope setting.
• Screen itself misses or delays touches: clean and dry the screen, remove a damaged protector, and check for touch-screen issues before changing sensitivity.
